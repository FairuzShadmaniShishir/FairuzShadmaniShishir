Projects

CIgFlow
PyTorch ESM-2 Flow Matching ProtGPT2 AlphaFold
Antigen-Conditioned Antibody Design via Conditional Flow Matching

CIgFlow is a generative framework that learns a continuous mapping from joint antibody-antigen embeddings to novel antibody sequences. It combines Conditional Flow Matching with a GPT-2-style autoregressive decoder, protein language model embeddings, and classifier-free guidance — generating antigen-specific antibodies while maintaining humanness and developability. The ODE-based latent sampler allows diverse exploration of sequence space without sacrificing quality.

NbBayesLM
PyTorch ESM-2 AbLang Bayesian NN
Bayesian Nanobody Thermostability Prediction with Calibrated Uncertainty

Predicts nanobody melting temperature (Tm) using ESM-2 and AbLang embeddings as input to a Bayesian deep learning framework that also encodes physicochemical features as priors. Evaluated on 10,000+ nanobody sequences: MAE of 1.89°C and R² of 0.67. The model decomposes epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty, letting researchers prioritize which candidates are actually worth synthesizing.

GitHub → Paper →
⚗️
GPCR-SLM
PyTorch Transformers Knowledge Distillation
Small Language Model for GPCR Classification (86 Families, 33.5× Speedup)

GPCRs are the target of ~35% of all approved drugs, but classifying them at scale is computationally expensive. GPCR-SLM uses knowledge distillation to compress large protein LM knowledge into a lightweight transformer that classifies 86 GPCR families at 99% accuracy while being 33.5× faster. Validated on clinical variants, metagenomic sequences, and tumor-derived data. Also supports open-set recognition for novel families.

Drug Toxicity
RDKit ChemBERT XGBoost SMILES
Multimodal Drug Toxicity Prediction Pipeline

A machine learning pipeline that combines SMILES representations, molecular fingerprints, and physicochemical descriptors with ChemBERT to predict drug-induced toxicity. Achieves 92% accuracy on cardiotoxicity prediction, significantly outperforming traditional QSAR baselines. Built as part of parameter-efficient deep learning work for computational drug discovery.

TensorFlow CNN-VAE WFDB Federated
Privacy-Preserving 12-Lead ECG Analysis for Cardiac Risk Prediction

A CNN-VAE architecture for analyzing 12-lead ECG signals that directly addresses demographic and acquisition bias — a known problem in cardiac AI that causes models to perform worse on underrepresented populations. Achieves 88% sensitivity and 94% specificity. Privacy-preserving by design. Published in Springer Nature Scientific Reports, 2026.
