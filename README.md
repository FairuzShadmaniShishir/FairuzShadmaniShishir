# Hi, I'm Fairuz Shadmani Shishir 👋
 
**AI/ML Scientist · Protein Design & Drug Discovery · Computational Biology**
 
PhD Candidate in Computer Science at the University of Kansas, building deep learning systems that accelerate therapeutic discovery — from generative antibody design to GPCR classification and cardiotoxicity prediction.
 
---
 
## 🔬 What I Work On
 
- **Generative Protein Design** — Flow matching & diffusion models for antigen-specific antibody generation (CIgFlow)
- **GPCR Classification** — Lightweight transformer achieving 99% accuracy across 86 families, validated on clinical & tumor-derived data (GPCR-SLM)
- **Drug Safety & ADMET** — hERG cardiotoxicity prediction combining molecular fingerprints and GNNs
- **Protein Function Prediction** — Metal binding site prediction and nanobody thermostability with calibrated uncertainty (MetaLLM, NbBayesLM)
- **Clinical AI** — Privacy-preserving ECG diagnostics in collaboration with KU Medical Center
